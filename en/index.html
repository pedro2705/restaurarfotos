    Before you send.

    How does payment work?

    You pay a 50% deposit via PayPal or Wise so I can start. Within 24 hours you get the preview. If you like it, I finish and you pay the other 50% on delivery.

    What if I don’t like the preview?

    I refund the full deposit. You only go ahead if you like the result.

    Do you use artificial intelligence?

    Yes, as a tool. The core work is manual digital editing. AI helps reframe, recover details and upscale the resolution for printing. Every result goes through my review.

    My photo is badly damaged. Can it be saved?

    In most cases, yes. Send it anyway: I assess it and tell you clearly what can be recovered and which level it fits, before you pay.

    Do I need to send the original photo?

    No. A good phone photo is enough, and the original stays with you. A scan is even better.

    How long does it take?

    The preview arrives within 24 hours of the deposit. The full photo is ready within 2 business days. Need it tomorrow? Rush delivery takes 24 hours.

    How do I receive the photo?

    By WhatsApp or email, in high resolution, ready to print, frame and share.

    Do you publish my photo?

    Only with your permission. Without it, the photo stays between us.

    Do you work with clients outside Brazil?

    Yes. Everything happens online, for clients anywhere in the world.

    Can I give it as a gift?

    Yes. There is a digital gift card with a personalized card, and the Tribute Package, the most popular gift.
